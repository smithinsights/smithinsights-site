Part Four

What Today's Average
ATO Member Is Like

More than 6,500 members have taken the Birkman Method since 2012, mapping how they behave and what they need to keep behaving that way.

The Birkman Method

One Visible Measure,
One Hidden Measure

Usual Behavior

How a member shows up when things are working for him. His default operating mode, visible and consistent to everyone around him.

Needs

What his environment has to provide for that behavior to hold. Almost always invisible to others, and rarely said out loud.

When needs go unmet, you get stress behavior. A chapter that does not know its members' needs reads that stress as a character problem.

The Average ATO Member

How He Shows Up,
and What He Needs

Usual Behavior

High social energy. Physically active. Outwardly confident. Action oriented and variety seeking. He leads by presence and momentum.

What He Needs

  • More time and information to process than he shows
  • Protection from criticism, which lands harder than it looks
  • Variety built into his environment
  • Financial commitments honored, both directions
  • Clarity on who is in charge and what is expected
