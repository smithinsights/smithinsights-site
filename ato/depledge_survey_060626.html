This update folds 5 new respondents and a refreshed database export into the depledge survey master, bringing the survey to 598 total responses (596 matched to a member record) against 65,645 cohort records. The analytical pipeline is unchanged from prior cycles: 23 rated departure-influence items reduce to five factors, tracked longitudinally against pledge cohort, cross-tabulated against whether the chapter offered a solution before the member left, and set against how long members actually stayed before departing.

Factor Structure

The 23 items reduce to five factors via exploratory factor analysis (varimax rotation, five retained). This item-to-factor mapping is held fixed across survey waves so factor scores stay comparable over time; it is not re-derived from each new batch of respondents. One correction this cycle: a third "Financial" item (bill accumulated to the point departure was easier) had never been assigned to a factor in prior documentation. It now joins Financial (Parent-Dependent) under F4, matching that factor's two-item, near-unity-alpha description.

Methods note: the environment's factor_analyzer/scikit-learn versions are incompatible this cycle (a removed check_array argument), so extraction fell back to eigendecomposition of the correlation matrix with varimax rotation, the same fallback this skill documents. That fallback recovers a dominant general-severity component rather than cleanly separating into the five conceptual factors item-for-item, so factor assignment below is the fixed instrument definition, not a fresh argmax of this cycle's loadings. Cronbach's alpha per factor is recomputed on current data and is the reliability evidence reported here.

F1
Organizational Fit and Culture
alpha = 0.837  ·  7 items
Organizational ConflictExperience GapMajor Organizational ChangeWorth the InvestmentSense of BelongingOrganization RulesPersonal Values Mismatch
The dominant factor in the instrument, capturing whether the member's experience, values, and sense of belonging matched what the chapter offered. It accounts for more shared variance among the 23 items than the other four factors combined, and it is the only factor with a statistically significant downward trend across pledge cohorts.
F3
Toxic Environment
alpha = 0.763  ·  6 items

Hazing, bullying, substance misuse, and a poor Coach-program experience. Grades suffering loads here as well, read as downstream academic impact of a toxic environment rather than an independent academic cause.
F2
Academic and Time Demands
alpha = 0.506  ·  3 items
Time CommitmentAcademics (Major/Balance Change)Financial (Own Pocket)
Time commitment, a change in major or difficulty balancing academics, and financial strain paid directly out of pocket. Reliability is modest for this factor; treat the composite as directional rather than a tight cluster.
F4
Financial Burden
alpha = 0.671  ·  2 items
Financial (Parent-Dependent)Financial (Bill Accumulated)
Parent-funded membership that became unaffordable, and a bill that accumulated to the point departure was easier than paying it down. Distinct from Financial (Own Pocket) in F2, which is the member paying directly.

alpha = 0.402  ·  3 items

Chapter house living, finding early what the member wanted from the experience, and religious fit. Reliability is below the conventional 0.60 threshold; interpret each item independently rather than as a unified construct.
Longitudinal Trends

Factor composite scores (0-4 scale, missing items excluded from each respondent's average) by pledge academic year. Where a solution was offered, F1 (Org Fit) and F3 (Toxic Environment) composites are markedly lower than where nothing was offered, consistent with successful intervention addressing culture and safety issues before they became fatal to the member's stay. F2 (Academic and Time) runs the other way: it is higher in the "solution offered" group, which reads as chapters intervening more readily on externally-caused departures (academics, time conflicts) than on internal culture problems.

Pledge Duration at Departure

Weeks between induction and departure for members whose status is Depledged (Status Reason is not used for this filter; it is unreliable pre-2019). Log scale; each cohort annotated with the percentage departing within the 8-week program threshold. 2025-26 is shown dimmed as an incomplete cohort.

Median time-to-departure has fallen from 20.4 weeks (2018-19 cohort) to 4.4 weeks (2025-26, incomplete), and the share departing within the first 8 weeks has risen from 12% to 68% over the same span (Spearman rho = -0.37, p < .001 across the 8-cohort window). Members who leave now do so far earlier in the pledge process than they did seven years ago.
