About

Chris Smith is the founder of Smith Insights, a data consulting practice built on a single mission: increase accuracy in the world. Since 2008, he has connected membership data and behavioral metrics to drive successful initiatives for fraternities, foundations, and the associations that serve them. His clients include the North American Interfraternity Conference (NIC) and the National Panhellenic Conference (NPC).

In addition to his consulting work, Chris serves as Director of Systems at Alpha Tau Omega National Fraternity and Director of Research for the Alpha Tau Omega Foundation, where he oversees membership data, predictive modeling, and donor research. He is also a certified Birkman Method consultant with over a decade of practice.

Chris holds a B.S. in Mathematics with a Professional Mathematics concentration from Middle Tennessee State University (MTSU) and is a member of Alpha Tau Omega Fraternity. Based in Atlanta, he works with clients nationwide.
